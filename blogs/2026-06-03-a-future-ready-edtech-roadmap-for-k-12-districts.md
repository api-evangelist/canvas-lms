School districts have accumulated fragmented technology stacks with minimal visibility into tool usage or impact on learning. The article argues that the smartest edtech strategy for the years ahead may be less, but better, advocating for consolidation around a unified learning platform rather than proliferation of disconnected applications. Canvas LMS is presented as the platform anchor for building a coherent, measurable edtech stack.
