Workforce Pell represents a highly prescriptive federal aid program requiring institutions to manage programs between 150 and 599 clock hours, demonstrate 70% completion and job placement rates, and maintain detailed compliance documentation by the July 1, 2026 effective date. Canvas Career is presented as purpose-built infrastructure to help institutions track these metrics efficiently, offering time-to-complete tracking, student engagement monitoring, date-restricted course structures, completion reporting, and skills-to-outcomes alignment. The article covers five specific ways Canvas Career supports federal Workforce Pell Grant compliance.
